# Trees Native to Raleigh, North Carolina

Raleigh sits in the **Piedmont region** of NC (USDA zones 7b/8a).

## Large Shade Trees

- **Oak spp.** (Quercus) — White oak, red oak, post oak, pin oak — all thrive in the Piedmont
- **Tulip Poplar** (Liriodendron tulipifera) — NC's tallest native, fast-growing, iconic yellow flowers
- **Black Gum** (Nyssa sylvatica) — great fall color, tolerates wet/dry, wildlife magnet
- **American Hornbeam** (Carpinus caroliniana) — musclewood, compact, great for smaller yards
- **Bald Cypress** (Taxodium distichum) — deciduous conifer, loves wet areas, gorgeous autumn

## Flowering & Ornamental

- **Flowering Dogwood** (Cornus florida) — spring favorite, white/pink bracts
- **Redbud** (Cercis canadensis) — purple-pink spring blooms
- **Serviceberry** (Amelanchier arborea) — white spring flowers, edible berries
- **Fringe Tree** (Chionanthus virginicus) — fragrant white flowers, underused gem

## Evergreens

- **Loblolly Pine** (Pinus taeda) — ubiquitous in the NC Piedmont
- **Eastern Red Cedar** (Juniperus virginiana) — dioecious, good for wildlife
- **American Holly** (Ilex opaca) — classic evergreen with red berries

## Understory / Smaller

- **Pawpaw** (Asimina triloba) — edible fruit, host for zebra swallowtail
- **Sassafras** (Sassafras albidum) — unique mitten-shaped leaves, great fall color
- **Green Hawthorn** (Crataegus viridis) — white spring blooms, persistent fruit
