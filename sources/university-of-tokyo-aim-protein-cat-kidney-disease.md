## Summary

This article details Professor Miyazaki's research at the University of Tokyo on the AIM protein (apoptosis inhibitor of macrophage) and its potential to treat kidney disease in cats. The research suggests that AIM-based treatments could potentially double cat lifespans from ~15 years to ~30 years by addressing the genetic dysfunction in feline AIM proteins.
